Common Inquiries

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Find answers to common questions about our clinical treatments, skincare equipment, software automation, and clinic partnerships.

What clinical devices and software integrations are supported by Skincare Suite?

Our suite integrates with medical-grade aesthetic equipment including multi-spectral skin analysis consoles, hydrodynamic vortex infusion wands, 28kHz ultrasonic cavitation spatulas, and clinical phototherapy arrays. On the software side, we support HL7/FHIR-compatible clinic databases, digital intake workflows, and custom formula compounding logs.

How do practitioners schedule equipment demonstrations or protocol trials?

You can schedule a clinical demonstration directly using the inquiry form on this page or through our consultation modal. Our clinical coordinator will confirm date availability, equipment configuration, and specialized protocol needs within two business hours.

Are operator training certifications and technical warranties provided?

Yes. All clinical hardware deployments include hands-on aesthetic practitioner certification modules, safety compliance checklists, and a comprehensive 24-month manufacturer warranty covering sensor calibration, fluidic lines, and priority parts replacement.

Can treatment protocols and mask compounding recipes be customized?

Absolutely. The workflow builder allows clinic directors to formulate bespoke treatment sequences, adjust active botanical infusion ratios, set contraindication safeguards, and tailor post-procedure patient recovery guidelines to their practice standards.

How does the platform ensure client health record security and compliance?

All client photographic diagnostics, consent documentation, and consultation records are secured with 256-bit encryption in transit and at rest. The platform maintains rigorous HIPAA and GDPR compliance with role-based access control and detailed audit logs.

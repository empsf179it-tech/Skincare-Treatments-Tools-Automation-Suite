THE TREATMENT COLLECTION

Every service integrates calibrated device settings with clinical-grade botanical formulations. Select a service to initiate an enquiry.

Purification 50 Mins • Cavitation

Ultrasonic Deep Pore Cleansing

28kHz ultrasonic sound waves aerosolize botanical cleansing fluids into microscopic bubbles, clearing sebum blockages and removing cellular debris without erythema.

Typical Process: Enzymatic warm steam softened prep, ultrasonic blade cavitation, gentle decongestant serum misting.
Key Consultation Question: Do you experience recurring T-zone congestion or micro-comedones around the nasal folds?
Barrier Recovery 60 Mins • Triple Hyaluronic

Intensive Hydration Infusion Facial

Infuses low, medium, and high molecular weight hyaluronic acids alongside ceramides to restore transepidermal water retention and relieve tight, dehydrated complexions.

Typical Process: Gentle pH-balanced exfoliation, ionized hyaluronic infusion, occlusive lipid barrier sealing.
Key Consultation Question: Does your skin feel dry or itchy after seasonal temperature changes or air travel?
Cellular Energy 45 Mins • Multi-Spectrum

Multi-Spectrum LED Phototherapy

Utilizes calibrated 633nm collagen-inducing red light and 415nm blemish-clearing blue light to calm superficial redness, accelerate tissue recovery, and promote skin clarity.

Typical Process: Double botanical cleanse, peptide prep layer, 25-minute calibrated LED exposure, soothing finish.
Key Consultation Question: Are you seeking post-procedure redness reduction or gentle ongoing collagen support?
Micro-Circulation 55 Mins • Lymphatic

Sculpting Lymphatic Facial Massage

Rhythmic manual contouring stimulates micro-lymphatic drainage, reducing fluid puffiness around the jawline and orbital bone while oxygenating facial muscular tissue.

Typical Process: Botanical cleansing melt, acupressure lymph opening, contouring myofascial strokes, cold stone press.
Key Consultation Question: Do you notice morning facial swelling or jaw clenching tension?
Diagnostics 35 Mins • Optical Lens

High-Magnification Optical Consultation

Close-up dermoscopic lens analysis examines barrier lipid integrity, follicular congestion patterns, and micro-capillary fragility to design your bespoke care protocol.

Typical Process: Skin history questionnaire review, multi-zone dermatoscope inspection, barrier health scoring.
Key Consultation Question: What active topical treatments or retinoids are currently in your daily skincare routine?
Long-term Regimen 45 Mins • 3D Scan Plan

Personalized Treatment Planning

Synthesizes multi-spectral imaging scores and lifestyle factors into a structured 6-month clinical roadmap, detailing recommended device sessions and companion homecare.

Typical Process: Multi-spectral baseline capture, 6-month interval projection, homecare compatibility matrix.
Key Consultation Question: Do you have an upcoming wedding or event timeline you are actively targeting?
